Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Jam Finder is a community tool that displays upcoming UK roller derby events and posts weekly event summaries to Instagram. This policy explains what data we handle and how.

What we collect

Jam Finder does not collect, store, or process any personal data from users who visit the website or view the Instagram account.

Event data is sourced from the publicly available rollerderby.directory calendar feed and contains only event names, locations, and dates — no personal information.

Instagram publishing

Jam Finder uses the Meta Graph API to automatically publish event summary posts to its own Instagram account. This integration:

  • Only posts to the Jam Finder Instagram account — it does not access any other Instagram account
  • Does not collect or store data from Instagram users
  • Does not use Instagram login or request any permissions from visitors

Cookies and tracking

Jam Finder does not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking technology.

Third-party services

  • rollerderby.directory — source of event data
  • Meta / Instagram Graph API — used to publish posts to the Jam Finder Instagram account
  • Railway — hosting provider
  • OpenStreetMap / Leaflet — map tiles displayed on the website

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, you can reach us via the Jam Finder Instagram account.
